# B2 — Information Architecture

**Purpose:** Proposed sitemap for the new Squarespace site. Every piece of content from the old site has a destination (or an explicit reason it was cut). Built on the B1 audit and the 18-parish peer research.

**Design principle:** A newcomer should find service times, what to expect, and "how do I show up?" in three clicks or fewer. A member should find the bulletin, the livestream, a sign-up, or a contact in two.

---

## Top-level navigation (5 items + actions)

```
[ Logo ]   Visit   Worship   Community   About   News & Events        [ Watch Live ]  [ Give ]
```

Five items in the main nav. Two persistent actions in the top-right: **Watch Live** (secondary, outlined) and **Give** (primary, filled). Mobile collapses to a hamburger.

Rationale: peer-site research shows **16/18 churches** surface a "Giving" CTA in header or hero and **12/18** surface "Watch Live" in hero or top nav. These aren't main-nav items — they're actions that live above everything else.

**10 items → 5 items.** Parent-level 404s are eliminated by reducing to categories that actually have landing pages.

## Homepage (/)

The one page most visitors will see. Every section below answers one of the five things newcomers look for.

| Section | Purpose | Key content |
|---|---|---|
| **Hero** | "You are welcome here" + when + where | Tagline, service times, CTA to Visit |
| **Welcome** | Who we are in 2 paragraphs | Warmth + mission + vision + "All are welcome" + brief rector-transition honesty |
| **Worship** | The two services, distinguished | 9:00 AM Contemporary + 11:10 AM Traditional, watch live, bulletin link |
| **Community life** | Four pillars with real photos | Invite / Nurture / Serve / Grow — brief + link to Community page |
| **Events & sign-ups** | 3–4 upcoming events | Card format, link to SignUpGenius, "see all" link |
| **Rector search update** | Honest transition status | During search only; removable post-call |
| **Connect / weekly word** | Newsletter + socials | Primary CTA: subscribe |
| **Footer** | Full contact + quick links | Address, phone, email, social, Realm, Lockhart, sitemap |

## /visit (Visit)

The newcomer front door. Replaces the current broken /im-new/ parent and consolidates /welcome-from-the-rector/, /service-hours/, /become-a-member/.

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Intro | "You are welcome here" — 2–3 sentences, not a wall of text |
| **Service times** | 9:00 AM Contemporary in Parish Hall, 11:10 AM Traditional in Nave + weekday online services |
| **What to expect** | First 15 minutes, what to wear, Communion practice, service length, where to sit |
| **Kids & family** | Nursery, children's sermon, Sunday School, family-friendly seating |
| **Directions & parking** | Address, map, parking (free lot on Lake Concord Rd), accessible entrances |
| **Welcome from the interim rector** | Short letter from Fr. John, signed |
| **First-time visit checklist** | Light touch: "Arrive → coffee → find a seat → worship → stay for fellowship" |
| **Become a member** | One paragraph with a link to /connect/ — not a separate page |

Peer-site research backs this design: **14/18 parishes** surface service days/times on the visitor page; **10/18** include kids/nursery; the rare-but-valuable additions (parking, what to wear, communion practice, where to enter) cluster on the strongest sites.

## Open items (to confirm with Nate during the build)

1. **Is Arboretum a page or a section of History?** It's niche but meaningful; recommend a section by default, a sub-page only if traffic warrants.
2. **Should Lockhart CDC get its own in-site page or just a footer link?** Recommend a one-paragraph in-site page at `/lockhart-cdc` that links externally — it gets Lockhart discoverability from the church site without maintaining two full sites.
3. **Do we keep a sermon archive on the site or just link to YouTube?** Recommend link-out. YouTube has better search, better mobile playback, and doesn't need to be rebuilt on our site.
4. **Annual report — in-site page or external link?** Recommend external (Flipsnack) with a link from `/news`. Same logic as sermons.
