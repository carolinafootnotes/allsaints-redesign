# Rector Search

*All Saints' is prayerfully searching for our next rector. This page is the place to follow the process — where we are, who is leading the work, and how the parish can take part. We are in good company: the Diocese of North Carolina walks with us, our Interim Rector serves alongside us, and the wider parish shapes the call.*

---

## Where we are now (status)

**Spring 2026.** The vestry has begun the formal search for our next rector under the guidance of the Diocese of North Carolina. The Rev. John Johanssen joined us as Interim Rector on January 1, 2026, and is helping the parish reflect on our common life and prepare for what is next. At our Annual Parish Meeting on January 25, 2026, the parish was invited to respond to two questions that will shape the parish profile and the call:

- *How can All Saints' become more effective in its ministry — personally and as a parish?*
- *What qualities do we most want in our next rector?*

Responses are still welcome. They are read with care and held in confidence as the committee shapes our parish profile.

**Where we are on the path:**

- **November 2025** — The Rev. Nancy Cox concluded her ministry as rector. The parish gave thanks for her years among us. [VERIFY: confirm Nancy Cox's name, retirement vs. transition wording, and exact month]
- **January 1, 2026** — The Rev. John Johanssen begins as Interim Rector.
- **January 25, 2026** — Annual Parish Meeting; the two discernment questions are introduced to the parish.
- **Early 2026** — The vestry, with Canon Marion Sprott (Canon for Transitions, Diocese of North Carolina), begins forming the Rector Search Committee. [VERIFY: current status of committee formation; chair and member names if/when public]
- **In development** — Parish profile (the Office of Transition Ministry portfolio that introduces All Saints' to candidates).
- **Next** — Position posted through the Diocese of North Carolina and the wider Episcopal Church; receipt of names; interviews; call.

A typical Episcopal rector search takes nine to eighteen months. We are not in a hurry. We would rather discern well than quickly.

## How to engage / contact

The search is the whole parish's work, and there are good ways for parishioners, candidates, and friends of All Saints' to take part.

**For parishioners — share your perspective.** If you have not yet responded to the two Annual Meeting questions, please do. Your answers shape the parish profile candidates will read. Submit through the [response form](https://allsaintsconcord.org/annual-meeting-questions/) or email **admin@allsaintsconcord.org**.

**For parishioners — pray with us.** A prayer for the search is offered in our Sunday liturgy and in the Weekly Word. We invite you to pray it at home as well.

**For clergy considering a call to All Saints'.** We welcome inquiries. Until the parish profile is published through the Office of Transition Ministry, the best first step is a note to our Senior Warden at **admin@allsaintsconcord.org** [VERIFY: preferred Search Committee inbox or Sr. Warden email; replace generic admin address if a dedicated one exists]. We will respond personally and will share the parish profile and OTM portfolio as soon as they are available.

**For diocesan colleagues and observers.** Our search is being conducted in partnership with the Diocese of North Carolina under The Rt. Rev. Samuel S. Rodman III, Bishop of North Carolina, with the support of Canon Marion Sprott, Canon for Transitions. [VERIFY: current Canon for Transitions; confirm Marion Sprott is still in role]

To learn more about Fr. John and our priest associates, visit the [Clergy](/about/clergy) page.
