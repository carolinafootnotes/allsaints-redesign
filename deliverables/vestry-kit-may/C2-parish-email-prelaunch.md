# C2 — Parish Email (Pre-launch)

**Send timing:** Start of the 2-week congregation preview window (mid-July, per timeline).
**Channel:** Constant Contact — whole parish list.
**Signed by:** Chuck Collier on behalf of the vestry. (Consider a short co-signed line from Rev. John as a grace note.)
**Subject line (primary):** A new allsaintsconcord.org — and we'd love your feedback
**Subject line (alt):** Take a look at our new website (before it goes live)

---

## Email body

**From:** Chuck Collier, Senior Warden · admin@allsaintsconcord.org
**To:** [Parish list via Constant Contact]
**Subject:** A new allsaintsconcord.org — and we'd love your feedback

---

Dear All Saints' family,

For the last few months, the vestry has been working on something small but overdue: a refreshed website. We're writing today because it's almost ready to go live, and we'd love you to look at it first.

**Why we're doing this.** Most people who meet All Saints' meet us online before they ever walk through the door. Our current site has served us well, but it's slow to update, hard to use on a phone, and — honestly — about a third of the menu links to pages that aren't there anymore. We've wanted to fix this for a while. The new site is simpler, works on a phone, and is much easier for our staff to keep current.

**What's changing.** The look and feel, the navigation (fewer menu items, clearer paths), and the photography (all real photos of our parish, our worship, and our campus). We've also built a dedicated page for the Rector Search so updates are easier to find as the process continues.

**What isn't changing.** Our address on the web (allsaintsconcord.org stays). Giving through Realm. The Weekly Word newsletter. The Realm member portal. The livestream on Facebook and YouTube. Our Sunday service times and our worship. Everything you know about All Saints' is exactly where you left it.

**We'd like your input.** Starting today and for the next two weeks, a preview of the new site is available at:

→ **[preview URL]**

Click around. See what works and what doesn't. If something feels off, a menu item is missing, or a page doesn't quite capture what we mean, please tell us:

→ **[feedback form URL]**

Every note will be read. Not every note will change the site — we can't promise that — but we'll let you know what we heard before we launch.

**When it goes live.** Assuming the preview window goes well, the new site launches in mid-August. Old bookmarks will redirect to the new locations for at least 30 days after that.

Thank you for your patience as we've been quiet about this. And thank you in advance for lending your eyes to help us get it right. A website is a small thing — but it's the first thing a lot of people meet when they meet All Saints'. We want it to reflect the community we know.

Grace and peace,

**Chuck Collier**
Senior Warden

*A note from Fr. John:* I've watched this project come together during my first months here, and I'm proud of the care the vestry has given it. Please do take the time to look and share what you see. — **JJ**

---

## Notes

- **Tone:** Warm, specific, a little self-aware about the old site's problems ("about a third of the menu links to pages that aren't there anymore") without being harsh.
- **What to customize before send:**
  - Preview URL (Squarespace staging site)
  - Feedback form URL (Google Form, or Squarespace native form)
  - Launch date ("mid-August" can tighten once scheduled)
  - Fr. John's note if he'd rather write his own line
- **Length:** ~290 words main body. Readable in under 90 seconds.
- **No images.** Plain text email will land higher in inboxes than a glossy HTML one; the preview URL is the "image."
- **Subject line A/B:** The first option ("A new allsaintsconcord.org — and we'd love your feedback") is warmer and clearer about the ask. The alternative is more functional. Default to option A.
