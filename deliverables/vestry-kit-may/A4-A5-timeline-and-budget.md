# Timeline & budget

**For the vestry — May 2026**
**Updated May 4, 2026 — launch pulled forward to mid-July.**

> Two questions in one document: when does this happen, and what does it cost?

---

## Timeline

Built around the vestry meeting cycle, with realistic buffer for volunteer-led work. Anything in **bold** is a vestry or review-group decision point.

| When | What happens | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Apr 21 — vestry meeting** | **Approve platform (Squarespace) + sign-ups (SignUpGenius). Design direction deferred to review group.** | Vestry |
| Late Apr – early May | Refresh content audit; lock IA; finalize three options' content | Nate |
| **May 4** | **Email review group with styleguide comparison + timeline + next steps** | Nate |
| **By Fri May 22** | **Review group sends gut-reaction feedback** | Review group |
| Late May | Synthesize input; converge on a direction | Nate |
| Late May – end of June | Subscribe to Squarespace Business; build the new site; migrate retained content | Nate |
| Late June | Internal review of staging (clergy + staff + review group) | Internal |
| **Late June – early July** | **Vestry sign-off on staging before congregation preview** | Vestry |
| Early July | Congregation preview window (~2 weeks) for feedback via simple form | Congregation |
| Early–mid July | Incorporate feedback; final polish | Nate |
| **Mid-July 2026** | **Launch.** New site goes live; old URLs redirect for 30 days | All |
| Mid August | 30-day check-in: address issues, hand off ongoing maintenance | Nate + staff |

**Why mid-July.** Gets the parish's best foot forward online during the rector search; lands before the program-year ramp-up in September; avoids any conflict with Holy Week or Easter prep. Compresses the build into a focused six-week window.

**What we're NOT doing.** We are not waiting for the new rector to arrive before launching. The Rector Search page is built to evolve; the new rector will shape welcome copy after they get to know us. Holding the website hostage to the search adds 3–6 months and no improvement.

## Budget

### Year-one cost

| Line item | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Squarespace Business plan | **$248** | $276 standard, less ~10% nonprofit discount, billed annually |
| Domain (allsaintsconcord.org) | **$0** | Free first year via Squarespace |
| SignUpGenius | **$0** | Free tier; sufficient for our needs |
| Constant Contact (Weekly Word) | **unchanged** | We already pay this |
| **Year-one total NEW spend** | **$248** | |

### Year-two and ongoing

| Line item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Squarespace Business plan | $276/year |
| Domain renewal | ~$15/year |
| **Annual total** | **~$291/year** |

### What we stop paying

| Line item | Estimated annual savings |
|---|---|
| WordPress hosting (depending on current host) | $100–250 |
| Premium plugins (security, backups, forms, SEO) | $150–300 |
| Theme renewals or developer fixes | $0–500 |
| **Estimated savings** | **$250–1,000/year** |

### Net impact

**Roughly cost-neutral** vs. WordPress, but we shed the maintenance burden and the "what happens if our one WordPress person leaves" risk.

### One-time costs (build phase)

If Nate builds: **$0**.

If we hire a Squarespace Circle freelancer: **$1,500–3,500 one-time**.

If a parish volunteer with Squarespace experience steps in: **$0**.

The vestry will only be asked to make decisions at these moments. Everything else is execution.
