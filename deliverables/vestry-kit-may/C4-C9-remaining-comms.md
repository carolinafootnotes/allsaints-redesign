# C4 — Pulpit announcement script

**Purpose:** 60-second announcement from the clergy during service announcements. Read on 2–3 consecutive Sundays leading into and during the congregation preview window, plus once on launch day.

**Who reads it:** Any of the clergy. If possible, Fr. John for at least one of the readings — it signals he's backing the project.

**Tone:** Warm, matter-of-fact, not salesy.

---

## Version 1 — Preview week opens (read 2 Sundays before launch)

> "Quick note on something you'll see in the bulletin and the Weekly Word this week. The vestry has been working on a refreshed website for All Saints'. It's now ready for your eyes before it goes live in August.
>
> Two reasons for the change: our current site doesn't work well on a phone, and frankly, a few of the menu items go to pages that aren't there anymore. The new one is simpler, works on a phone, and is easier for our staff to keep up to date.
>
> A preview link is in the Weekly Word and in the bulletin today. Take a look. Tell us what works and what doesn't. The form for feedback is in the same place. We'd rather hear about a typo or a missing menu item now than after it's live.
>
> Thank you for being the kind of parish that gets asked these questions, and answers them."

*(~45 seconds read at a conversational pace.)*

---

## Version 2 — Launch Sunday (read the Sunday of launch or the following)

> "You may have noticed our website looks different this week. After a couple of months of work and a good round of feedback from many of you, the new allsaintsconcord.org is live.
>
> Same address, new design. Everything you need — service times, the livestream, giving, the bulletin — is easier to find, especially on a phone.
>
> If you bookmarked a page and it's suddenly somewhere else, it'll redirect for the next 30 days while you update. And if you can't find something at all, there's a feedback form linked on every page. Tell us. We'll fix it.
>
> Thank you for your patience, and thank you to everyone who clicked around during the preview and sent notes. The site is better because of you."

*(~40 seconds.)*

## Notes on C5

- **Acknowledgment matters.** The thank-you paragraph names specific people and roles. Parishioners notice when their work is seen.
- **Keep the "what isn't changing" list.** Long-time members want reassurance that the familiar stays familiar.
- **No screenshots in the email.** A link is warmer than a tour. Let them discover for themselves.
- **Length:** ~290 words main body.

# C6 — Congregation FAQ

**Where this lives:** A hidden page on the site linked from the launch email, the bulletin, and the footer. Not in the main navigation. Also pasteable directly into the Weekly Word if needed.

**Format:** Expandable Q&A style or simple list. Keep answers short — 2–3 sentences each.

---

## Why did we change our website?

Our previous site worked, but it was getting harder to maintain and didn't work well on phones. About a third of the menu items led to pages that weren't there anymore. The new site is simpler, mobile-friendly, and easier for our staff to keep up to date.

## Is this a re-brand?

No. Our logo, our colors, our name, our mission, our vision, and everything about who we are is unchanged. The only thing that's different is how our website looks and works.

## What happened to my bookmarks?

Old bookmarks will redirect to the new pages automatically for at least 30 days. After that, if you land on a broken link, the site will point you to the new homepage. Bookmarks to the really popular things — giving, the livestream, the bulletin — should still work indefinitely.

## Where is giving?

Same place it's always been: Realm. The "Give" button at the top of every page takes you there. Your giving history, recurring gifts, and pledges are all untouched.

## Where is the livestream?

On Facebook Live and YouTube, exactly like before. The "Watch Live" button at the top of every page is the fastest path.

## Where is the Weekly Word?

Still coming from Constant Contact, exactly as it did before. Nothing about your subscription has changed. You can also find recent issues on the News & Events page.

## Where is the weekly bulletin?

On the Worship page. The current week's bulletin is always linked there, updated each Friday.

## Where is the member portal / Realm?

Same URL as before. There's a link in the footer of every page, labeled "Member Portal."

## Where is Lockhart CDC?

Lockhart has its own website at lockhartcdc.org — that hasn't changed. You'll find a link to it in our footer and a short introduction page on our site.

## How do I sign up for an event now?

On the News & Events page. Every event that needs a sign-up has a card with a "Sign up" button that opens our SignUpGenius page for that event. Same experience, in one consistent place.

## Who can I ask if I can't find something?

The contact form on the Visit page reaches our parish office directly. You can also email **admin@allsaintsconcord.org** or call **(704) 782-2024**. And during the first month after launch, a feedback form is linked in the footer of every page.

## Will the website keep improving?

Yes. Small updates are now easy for staff to make without a developer. If you notice something we should fix, tell us. If you have a photo of parish life we should add, send it to Brian Sapp-Moore.

## What about the Rector Search?

The Rector Search has a dedicated page under About. It's updated as the process moves forward, with the most recent status always at the top. If you haven't shared your perspectives yet, there's a form linked from that page.

## Who made these decisions?

The vestry approved the direction and platform at the April 21, 2026 meeting. The design was one of three options the parish has been able to review and comment on since the January Annual Meeting. Fr. John and the clergy team signed off on tone and theology. Nate Ernst led the project.

## How much did this cost?

Roughly **$248** the first year and **$276** per year after that — about what we were paying for WordPress hosting and plugins combined. Fr. John and the clergy team's time, and Nate's time, were volunteered.

# C9 — Preview-period feedback form (spec)

**Tool:** Google Form (simplest) OR Squarespace native form (if we want to keep everything in one place).
**Recommendation:** Squarespace form for consistency. Google Form if we want the responses to flow into a shared sheet staff can triage easily.

**Where it lives:**
- Linked in the footer of every preview page
- Linked in the parish email (C2)
- Linked in the pulpit announcement (C4)
- Linked in the Weekly Word blurb (C7)

---

## Form fields

1. **What's your name?** *(optional — signaled as such)*
   - Short text

2. **Did you find what you were looking for?**
   - Radio: Yes / Mostly / No / I wasn't looking for anything specific

3. **What were you looking for?** *(optional)*
   - Short text

4. **What page were you on when you had the thought you're telling us about?** *(optional)*
   - Short text

5. **What should we know?** *(the main field)*
   - Long text, required
   - Placeholder: "Something that broke? Something missing? A photo we should add? A typo? Tell us."

6. **Can we follow up with you about this?**
   - Radio: Yes, here's my email / No need
   - Conditional email field if yes

7. **Anything else?** *(optional)*
   - Long text

---

## Form response handling

- **Auto-reply:** "Thanks — we'll read every note and reply within a week."
- **Staff triage:** Ruth Brown checks responses weekly; Nate reviews all responses weekly during the preview window.
- **Nothing gets dismissed without a reply**, even if the response is "we considered this but chose not to change it."

---

## Form page header copy

> **Help us get this right.**
>
> You're looking at a preview of our new website. It's not quite live yet. If you found something confusing, something missing, or just a typo — tell us. Every note is read. Most will shape what we ship.
