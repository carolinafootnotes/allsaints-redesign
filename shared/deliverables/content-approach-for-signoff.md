# Content approach for the new site
What I'm asking you to sign off on

## The short version
We're moving All Saints' off WordPress onto Squarespace. The cleanest path: bring the pages we've already designed across as the new live site, fold in the handful of older items still worth keeping, and quietly retire the long tail of old posts and files. I'd like the group's blessing on the approach before I start the import.

## What we have today
The current site has about 70 pages, 487 published blog posts, another 144 drafts, and roughly 2,462 attached images, PDFs, and files. A lot of that is years of announcements, event recaps, COVID-era popups, and old curriculum sessions that nobody reads anymore.

We've also identified 43 specific URLs that have to keep working at the exact same address. These are the ones with physical dependencies: memorial tree pages linked from QR codes on the trees themselves, the arboretum, the labyrinth, and the prayer request form printed in handouts.

## What I'm proposing, three buckets

**Active content.** The pages we've been reviewing together become the new live site. Plus a small, curated set of sermons and a few evergreen pieces (rector's welcome, history, etc.) that still belong.

**Preserved URLs.** Those 43 addresses keep working exactly as they do now, either as a real page on the new site or as an automatic forward. QR codes on the memorial trees, the arboretum signs, and printed handouts all keep working without anyone touching them.

**Archived or retired.** Most of the older blog posts and most of the older photo and file library don't come along. Some of it gets retired completely. If there's anything worth keeping for the record, I can park it in a read-only archive at a separate address so it still exists but isn't part of the new site.

## What it means for the group
You won't lose anything you'd notice. The pages you've reviewed are the new live site. Most old posts aren't disappearing in an ugly way, they're either folded in where they still matter or quietly retired. Memorial tree pages, the arboretum, and the labyrinth keep their exact addresses, so the QR codes on the signs don't break.

## What I'm asking
1. Are you comfortable with most old blog posts being retired rather than migrated?
2. Are you comfortable with the older photo and file library not coming along, keeping only what's actively used on current pages?
3. Do you trust the 43-URL preservation list, or are there URLs out in the world (bulletins, signs, business cards) that I should add?

## What you're NOT being asked to do
You don't need to pick which posts to keep. You don't need to look through the file library. You don't need to understand the technical side. I'm asking you to bless the approach, and I'll handle the work.

## What happens after sign-off
I clean up the WordPress export, import the cleaned content into Squarespace, set up the URL forwards, and then start sending you the page-by-page approval packets. Those packets are where your real input lands.
