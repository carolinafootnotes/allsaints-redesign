# C3 — Sunday Bulletin Blurb

**Purpose:** Short announcement repeated in the printed Sunday bulletin for 3–4 weeks leading up to launch, then once more the week of launch.
**Length:** 2–4 sentences. Fits in an announcements column without eating space.
**Style:** Same voice as the parish email. Short enough to read in 15 seconds.

---

## Primary version — weeks 3–4 before launch

> **A new website is on its way.** For the past few months the vestry has been working on a refreshed allsaintsconcord.org — cleaner, easier on a phone, and simpler for our staff to keep current. A preview will be shared with the parish in the coming weeks; we'll want your eyes on it before it goes live in August.

---

## Preview-week version — 2 weeks before launch

> **Our new website is open for your review.** Take a look at [short URL / QR code] and share what you find — what works, what doesn't, what's missing. The feedback form is at [short URL]. Thank you for helping us get it right before launch in mid-August.

---

## Launch-week version

> **Welcome to the new allsaintsconcord.org.** Our refreshed website is live today. Bookmarks to old pages will redirect for the next 30 days. If you can't find something, tell us at [feedback URL] — we'll fix it.

---

## Format notes for the bulletin producer

- **Boldface the lead sentence** so it catches the eye in a crowded announcements section.
- **If space is tight**, the first sentence alone works. The rest is nice-to-have.
- **Include a QR code** for the preview and launch-week versions — most parishioners are reading the bulletin with a phone nearby.
- **Don't run all three versions in the same bulletin.** One at a time, in sequence.

## Suggested cadence

| Week | Version | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 4 weeks before launch | Primary | First mention; sets expectation |
| 3 weeks before launch | Primary | Repeat for latecomers |
| 2 weeks before launch | Preview-week | Preview opens; feedback form live |
| 1 week before launch | Preview-week | Last call for feedback |
| Launch week | Launch-week | Celebrate the go-live |
| Week after launch | (optional) | Thank-you mention if feedback was substantial |
