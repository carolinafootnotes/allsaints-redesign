## Hero / opening (1 paragraph)

Generosity sustains everything we do at All Saints' — Sunday worship, music, formation for children and adults, pastoral care for those in need, and our outreach into the wider community of Concord. Thank you for being part of it.

## How to give

**Online (preferred).** Give securely through Realm, our parish management system. You can give one time or set up a recurring gift, and your giving history is always available to you. **[Give online →]** *(Button links to existing Realm giving URL)*

525 Lake Concord Road NE
Concord, NC 28025

**In the offering plate** during either Sunday liturgy.

## Stewardship

Each fall, the parish invites pledges for the coming year. A pledge is a planned commitment that lets the vestry build a responsible budget for ministry — staff, music, formation, outreach, the building itself. If you'd like to learn more about pledging or update an existing pledge, please contact the parish office.
