# Social Media Content Samples

A reference doc showing the 80/20 rotation in practice. Not for the deck — for you to point to during the April 20 conversation if anyone asks "what does this actually look like?"

Platform split is the same on both Instagram and Facebook. Facebook gets the same copy with a slightly longer caption where it helps. Instagram is image-first.

---

## The scheduled 75-80% (evergreen, rotating)

### Episcopal Fact Monday — 1st and 3rd Mondays

**Image:** Clean quote card. Cream background, burgundy text, small gold Episcopal shield in a corner.

**Caption:**
> Episcopal 101: The Episcopal Church has ordained women as priests since 1976 and as bishops since 1989. Today, about 1 in 4 Episcopal clergy are women.
>
> If you've wondered what makes the Episcopal tradition what it is, we'll be sharing one small fact every other Monday.
>
> #Episcopal #AllSaintsConcord

**Why it works:** Low-commitment entry point. Someone searching "progressive church Concord NC" sees we're a real denomination with real history — not a startup.

---

### Parish History Tuesday — 2nd and 4th Tuesdays

**Image:** Archival photo from the parish, or a recent photo of a specific place (the labyrinth, the bell garden, a stained glass panel).

**Caption:**
> This is the labyrinth in our bell garden. It's modeled on the medieval pattern at Chartres Cathedral in France — a walking meditation that takes about 15 minutes.
>
> It's open whenever the church is open. You don't need to be a member to walk it.
>
> #AllSaintsConcord #ConcordNC

**Why it works:** Gives a specific, visitable thing. Shows the parish is a physical place with texture, not just a building.

---

### Episcopal Media Wednesday — 3 Wednesdays/month

**Image:** Screenshot or cover art from whatever you're pointing to — an article, a podcast episode, a video from the national church.

**Caption:**
> Worth a read: The Living Church recently profiled the Episcopal parishes that have grown fastest over the last decade. The common thread wasn't flashy programs — it was clarity about identity and a real welcome.
>
> Link in bio.
>
> #Episcopal #ChurchGrowth

**Why it works:** Positions the parish as part of a broader Episcopal conversation. People searching for "affirming Episcopal" find you connected to credible voices.

---

### Ministry Spotlight — once a month

**Image:** A photo of the ministry in action. Real people, real faces. Not stock.

**Caption:**
> Meet the Altar Guild. Every Saturday, a small team prepares the sanctuary for Sunday — polishing the brass, changing the linens, arranging the flowers. It's quiet, steady work that almost nobody sees.
>
> If you've ever wondered how the church gets ready for you, this is how.
>
> Want to learn more? Reach out through our website.
>
> #AllSaintsConcord #ChurchLife

**Why it works:** Shows the parish runs on volunteers, not a paid staff. Invites participation without pressure.

## The ad-hoc 20-25% (weekly, timely, human)

### Weekly service preview — every Friday or Saturday

**Image:** Photo of the interior, or a detail from a seasonal flower arrangement.

**Caption:**
> This Sunday: The Third Sunday of Easter. Holy Eucharist at 10:30am. Coffee hour after.
>
> All are welcome — whoever you are and wherever you are on your journey of faith.
>
> #AllSaintsConcord

**Why it works:** Direct, findable. Someone looking for a church to try this weekend gets the answer in two lines.

---

### Event or milestone — when something's happening

**Image:** Photo from the event if it's already happened, or a clean flyer graphic if it's upcoming.

**Caption:**
> Thank you to everyone who came out for our Earth Day service in the bell garden this morning. Fifty people, one big oak tree, and a good reminder that creation care is part of the work.
>
> #AllSaintsConcord #EarthDay

**Why it works:** Shows the parish is active and present. People who weren't there see what they missed and get a nudge for next time.

Plus 1-2 ad-hoc posts a month for events, milestones, or timely news. That's roughly 12-14 posts per month, about 3 a week — enough to show up, not so much that it becomes a second job.

---

## A note on the "affirming" question

None of the sample posts above say "affirming." That's intentional. The strategy isn't to hide it — it's to let people find it through the door they opened themselves. When someone searches for "affirming Episcopal Concord NC," our About page is the landing. The feed shows what we do; the website confirms who we welcome.

If you want a specific affirming post, the highest-impact moment is Pride month — a simple, confident post, not a debate. Everything else can do its work quietly.
