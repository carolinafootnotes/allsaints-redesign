# Haiti — is it still an active mission, and how should the new site say so?

## The question (one sentence)

Does All Saints' currently have an active Haiti mission, and how should the new website represent our relationship with Haiti?

## Why we're asking

This surfaced while triaging the old `/play/` page for migration. The website tells three different and partly contradictory stories about Haiti, and the new site needs one clear, honest, evergreen answer.

What the existing content actually says:

- **The old "In the World" page (2015)** describes Haiti as All Saints' first-ever overseas mission, starting with a 1996 trip to the CODEP reforestation project in the Cormeir, Bwa Goch, and Gros Morn valleys. Teams traveled to do construction, plumbing, and carpentry. It describes regular trips and shipping containers of materials.
- **The old `/play/` page** had a "Hops Helping Haiti" BBQ-and-beer fundraiser whose proceeds funded "our Haiti mission team, which is planning a trip for 2016." Nothing more recent than that.
- **The most recent Haiti content anywhere on the site is March 2025, "Remembering Haiti" by the Rev. Vern Cahoon.** It is not a mission-team update or a trip announcement. It is a Lenten reflection on Bishop James Theodore Holly that asks the parish to *pray* for Haiti amid the gang-violence crisis and directs financial gifts to **Episcopal Relief & Development**, not to a CODEP team trip.

So the public record shows organized trips and the Hops fundraiser around 2015–2016, then a long gap, then a 2025 shift in tone toward prayer and giving through a national agency. The last trip the old site actually describes was 2007. Real-world context: Haiti has been effectively closed to mission travel for years due to the security situation.

**Important flag:** the new Serve page we've built currently presents Haiti in the present tense ("mission since 1996 in the Cormeir, Bwa Goch, and Gros Morn valleys... around 600 local families... Haiti CODEP"). That copy is accurate to the parish's own historical content, but if trips and active support have wound down, the present-tense framing overstates current activity. We should not let it go live until the group confirms the real status.

## What we need from you

A read on where Haiti stands today, then we'll match the site to it. The likely options:

### A. Still an active mission

CODEP support and/or trips are current. We keep the present-tense framing on Serve, and we can revive "Hops Helping Haiti" as a fellowship fundraiser elsewhere on the site.

### B. A giving and prayer relationship, no active trips

We still care about and support Haiti, but organized trips have stopped. We reframe Serve to "we support the work in Haiti through prayer and giving" (CODEP and/or Episcopal Relief & Development), drop the trip language, and do not revive Hops.

### C. Part of our history, not a current mission

Haiti involvement has wound down. We tell it honestly as part of who we've been (history page), and remove it from the active outreach list on Serve.

### D. Something else: [your description]

## One concrete fact that would settle most of this

Has anyone from All Saints' traveled to Haiti, or has the parish sent designated financial support to CODEP or a Haiti mission, in roughly the last two to three years? A yes-or-no on that, plus whoever currently shepherds the relationship (the Rev. Vern Cahoon wrote the 2025 piece and may know), tells us which option above is honest.
