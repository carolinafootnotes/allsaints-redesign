# Content audit — /final home vs. v1 home

**Reviewer:** Senior UX (product design team)
**Reference URLs:**
- New: https://allsaints-redesign.nate-ernst7.workers.dev/final
- v1: https://allsaints-redesign.nate-ernst7.workers.dev/v1

**How to use this doc:** Each item shows the current /final copy, an "Adopt from v1" alternative, and the team's one-line rationale. Mark each item with your decision. Where the recommendation conflicts with a prior decision you made, I've flagged it in a note.

The Visit page is excluded — it was already rebuilt with v1's content depth.

---

## 1. Hero subtitle

**Current /final:**
> A vibrant, welcoming community where everyone has a place at the table. Rooted in tradition. Alive in the Spirit.

**Better option (v1):**
> A faith community where Jesus' love, forgiveness, and healing is changing lives. Join us this Sunday.

**Why:** v1 names what's actually happening to people; /final's version describes the church's self-image with generic adjectives ("vibrant", "alive in the Spirit") that mean nothing concrete.

Decision: ☐ keep ☐ adopt ☐ hybrid

---

## 2. Welcome block, first paragraph

**Current /final:**
> At All Saints, you won't find a perfect church. You'll find a real one, full of people learning to love God and each other, one Sunday at a time. We've been gathering in Concord since 1878, and the door is wide open for you.

**Better option (v1-derived hybrid):**
> Whether you are visiting from afar or have lived in Concord your whole life, you are welcome here. We are a faith community where God's love meets you exactly where you are.

**Why:** "You won't find a perfect church" is a cliche that forces the reader to sit with a negative before reaching the positive; v1 leads with direct welcome and grounds it in place.

> **My note:** This is praised copy from earlier sessions — you've explicitly defended the "real church" framing. The team is suggesting a real downgrade in distinctiveness. I'd lean keep, but flagging.

Decision: ☐ keep ☐ adopt ☐ hybrid

---

## 3. Welcome block — add a "first-visit practical info" paragraph

**Current /final:** scatters service times, location, kids, accessibility across multiple sections.

**Better option (v1):**
> Our 9 AM service in the Parish Hall is casual and family-friendly. Our 11:10 AM service in the Nave features choir and organ. Both are Holy Eucharist, Rite II, and both are livestreamed. Nursery available for infants and toddlers. Accessible entrances on all sides.

**Why:** v1 puts all first-visit practical info in one paragraph inside the Welcome block, where a newcomer needs it before scrolling. /final hides it across worship + visit cards.

Decision: ☐ keep ☐ adopt ☐ hybrid

## 4. Welcome block, Diocese paragraph

**Current /final:**
> We are a parish of the Episcopal Diocese of North Carolina. The Rev. John Johanssen serves as our Interim Rector while the vestry's search for our next rector is underway.

**Better option (v1):**
> A parish of the Episcopal Diocese of North Carolina since 1878. The Rev. Nancy Cox served as our rector through 2025; The Rev. John Johanssen began as Interim Rector on January 1, 2026, while the vestry's search for our next rector is underway.

**Why:** v1's version tells the full transition story instead of pretending the current state is natural; the context makes Fr. John feel like a trusted bridge rather than an unexplained placeholder.

> **My note:** This **reverses item #8 from this morning's batch**, where you decided to drop the Nancy Cox mention. Worth re-debating: foreground the present (your earlier call) vs. tell the full story (UX's call). Episcopal precedent goes both ways.

Decision: ☐ keep ☐ adopt ☐ hybrid

## 5. Stats row, fourth stat

**Current /final:** `2`  ·  `Books We Gather Around`

**Better option:** `5`  ·  `Music Ensembles`  (currently buried in the Rector Search section body copy)

## 6. Worship section subtitle

**Current /final heading:** "Two Services. One Community."
**Current /final sub:** "However you connect with God, there's a service that feels like home."

**Better option (UX rewrite):**
> Both services are Holy Eucharist, Rite II. One is casual and family-friendly; one is traditional with choir and organ. Both are real.

**Why:** "feels like home" is filler. Naming the actual liturgical form (Rite II) and what's distinct about each is more honest and gives newcomers real information.

Decision: ☐ keep ☐ adopt ☐ hybrid

## 14. Connect CTA body copy

**Current /final:**
> Whether you're ready to walk through the door this Sunday or just want to follow along from afar, we'd love to stay in touch.

**Better option (v1):**
> Whether you want to worship, volunteer, join a small group, or simply find community, there is a place for you here.

**Why:** v1 lists specific forms of belonging (worship, volunteer, small group, community). /final's "follow along from afar" positions the reader as a passive observer and undersells the invitation.

Decision: ☐ keep ☐ adopt ☐ hybrid

## 15. Connect CTA bottom button

**Current /final:** five social cards + a bottom button "Join Us This Sunday" (which duplicates the hero button).

**Better option (v1):** two-button structure — `Connect With Us` + `Support Our Mission`

**Why:** the bottom of the Connect section shouldn't repeat the "show up Sunday" push from the hero. It's where people who've already decided to engage go deeper. v1's two buttons serve newcomers (Connect) and existing members (Support) distinctly.

Decision: ☐ keep ☐ adopt ☐ hybrid
