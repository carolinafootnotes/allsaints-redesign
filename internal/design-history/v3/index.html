Welcome Home

A Church That Loves Like
God Loves

"God is love, and those who abide in love abide in God, and God abides in them."
— 1 John 4:16

At All Saints, you won't find a perfect church. You'll find a real one — full of people learning to love God and each other, one Sunday at a time. We've been gathering in Concord since 1878, and the door is wide open for you.

Our mission is simple: Invite, Nurture, Serve, and Grow. Whether you're exploring faith for the first time or you've been in the pew your whole life, this is your place.

We are a parish of the Episcopal Diocese of North Carolina. The Rev. Nancy Cox served as our rector through 2025; The Rev. John Johanssen began as Interim Rector on January 1, 2026, while the vestry's search for our next rector is underway.

What to expect when you visit

The shape of the service. A bulletin walks you through everything — when to stand, sit, kneel, or stay seated. The Book of Common Prayer is in the pew if you'd like to follow along.
Communion. All who love God and are drawn to Christ are welcome at Christ's table — you don't need to be Episcopalian or a member here. Cross your arms for a blessing if you'd rather not receive.
What to wear. Whatever you're comfortable in. Both services run about an hour to an hour and fifteen minutes.
Children. Children are a full part of worship here. Nursery care is available; formation for kids meets between services.

Where We Are

Our Rector Search

All Saints' is in a season of careful discernment as we listen for the next priest called to lead this parish.

Where we are now

The Rev. Nancy Cox served All Saints' faithfully as rector through the end of 2025. The Rev. John Johanssen — an experienced interim with specialized training in transition ministry and a Master of Divinity from Virginia Theological Seminary — began as our Interim Rector on January 1, 2026.

With Fr. John's steady leadership, the vestry has begun the work of calling our next rector, walking together through the discernment process in partnership with the Episcopal Diocese of North Carolina.

For clergy considering this call: we are a 1878-rooted parish in Concord, NC, with two Sunday liturgies, five named music ensembles, formation for every age, and a real, mature congregation. We would welcome the conversation.

To contact the search committee or express interest:
