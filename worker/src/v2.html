Who We Are

Come as you are. Stay as family.

All Saints is a place where questions are welcome, where the bread and wine are shared with open hands, and where the ancient rhythms of prayer meet the real, everyday lives of people in Concord.

Whether you have been Episcopalian your whole life or have never set foot in a church, there is a seat at this table for you. We have been gathering on this ground since 1878 — and the door is always open.

A parish of the Episcopal Diocese of North Carolina. The Rev. Nancy Cox served as our rector through 2025; The Rev. John Johanssen began as Interim Rector on January 1, 2026, while the vestry's search for our next rector is underway.

Sunday Worship

Two services, one spirit

Each Sunday we gather in two distinct ways. Both are rooted in the same faith, both welcome you exactly as you are, and both are livestreamed for those joining from home.

9:00 AM Livestreamed

Family Eucharist

Relaxed, joyful, family-centered

Held in our Parish Hall, this service has the energy of a family gathering. Expect praise music, a children's sermon that adults secretly love, and a come-as-you-are atmosphere. Coffee is always nearby.

11:10 AM Livestreamed

Traditional Eucharist

Reverent, beautiful, steeped in tradition

In our Nave, with sunlight streaming through stained glass, the choir lifts ancient hymns and the organ fills the space. This is Rite II from the Book of Common Prayer — liturgy that has shaped Christian worship for centuries.

Community Life

Faith is a practice, not a spectator sport

We organize our common life around four movements. Together, they shape us into the kind of community we believe God calls us to be.

01

Invite

Opening doors and hearts

Before we teach, serve, or grow, we welcome. This is a church where strangers become friends before they become members, where the only prerequisite for belonging is showing up.

Newcomers' Welcome: Join us on the first Sunday of each month after the 9 AM service for coffee, conversation, and the chance to meet people who were once newcomers themselves.
02

Nurture

Growing in faith together

Faith deepens through conversation, study, and honest questions. From Bible study to Children & Youth ministries to two Education for Ministry (EfM) groups, there is always a place to grow.

Christian Formation: Weekly classes for adults, children, and youth at 10:10 AM between services — a natural time to dig deeper.
03

Serve

Caring for our neighbors

Our faith means nothing if it stays inside these walls. We feed the hungry, partner with Episcopal Relief & Development, and show up for our neighbors through pastoral care.

Community Kitchen: Every month, our volunteers prepare and serve meals to families in need — no questions asked, just food and fellowship.
Make a joyful noise
04

Grow

Becoming more like Christ

Growth happens in prayer, in worship, and in the arts. Five named music ensembles shape our worship at every age — from our youngest singers to the full choir at the 11:10 liturgy.

Music Ministry: Choir of All Saints, Resurrection Choir, Bells of All Saints, and the St. Nicholas and Junior Choirs for our youngest singers.

Your first time should feel easy

What to Expect

  • Parking: Free lot right on Lake Concord Road. You will see the signs. Accessible spaces are near the main entrance.
  • Dress code: Come as you are. Some folks wear jeans, some wear suits. You will fit in either way.
  • Kids: Nursery care for infants and toddlers at both services. Children's sermon at 9 AM. They are not just welcome — they are wanted.
  • Accessibility: Accessible entrances are available. An usher will be happy to help you find your way.
  • During the service: Do not worry about knowing when to sit, stand, or kneel. Everything is in the bulletin, and the person next to you will be glad to help.

Where We Are

Our Rector Search

All Saints' is in a season of careful discernment as we listen for the next priest called to lead this parish.

The Rev. Nancy Cox served All Saints' faithfully as rector through the end of 2025. The Rev. John Johanssen — an experienced interim with specialized training in transition ministry and a Master of Divinity from Virginia Theological Seminary — began as our Interim Rector on January 1, 2026.

With Fr. John's steady leadership, the vestry has begun the work of calling our next rector, walking together through the discernment process in partnership with the Episcopal Diocese of North Carolina.

For clergy considering this call: we are an 1878-rooted parish in Concord, NC, with two Sunday liturgies, five named music ensembles, formation for every age, and a real, mature congregation. We would welcome the conversation.

To contact the search committee or express interest: admin@allsaintsconcord.org
