Website redesign review · May 2026

Which one best represents All Saints at this moment?

Highlighting the elements that shape how each design feels.

There is no wrong answer. We are picking a feeling, not locking into a final design — we can adjust the wording no matter which direction we go.

Think of a new family or a new rector checking us out online. How do we want the parish to come across?

The Sanctuary

🕯️Reverent
See it live →
One long page. Everything lives on a single homepage that scrolls down through Worship, Community, Clergy, and Visit — like reading a story top to bottom.
Color
Warm parchment & deeper burgundy. A candlelit, prayer-book mood. The cream is warmer; gold is used sparingly for accents.
Typography
Come as you are. Stay as family.
All Saints is a place where questions are welcome and the bread and wine are shared with open hands.
