All Saints' Episcopal Church — Site Map

Every page on the new website, and how people reach it
Draft for review · Detailed version · June 2026

This is a plain outline of the new site, organized by how people get to each page. It is a reference, not the finished design. The pages themselves are at the preview link in the email. A second, names-only version of this map is also attached.

The Top Menu (visible on every page)
These are the main buttons across the top of the site. The home page is the All Saints' logo.
  • Home The front door, reached by clicking the logo
  • Visit What to expect on a Sunday morning: service times and the shape of worship
  • About Who we are, with four pages beneath it:
    • Clergy & StaffMeet the priests and parish staff
    • Our HistoryAll Saints' since 1878, with a digital edition of the parish history book
    • Formation & LearningChildren, youth, adults, and Education for Ministry
    • Memorial ArboretumThe memorial grounds: 36 memorial trees and the prayer labyrinth, each reachable by its sign's QR code (web addresses kept as they are today)
  • Connect Ways to belong, with three pages beneath it:
    • New to the Episcopal ChurchFor people unfamiliar with the tradition
    • Life EventsBaptisms, weddings, funerals
    • Pastoral CareSupport in times of need
  • Serve How the parish serves locally and beyond
  • Rector Search For clergy considering this call (retires after the search)
  • Give Opens the parish's Giving page: ways to give, annual estimated giving, Tour de Saints, and a link to secure online giving:
    • Online giving (Realm) ↗Secure giving portal, opens outside the site
Reached Through the Footer
Every page has the same footer at the bottom, with links to these pages.
  • What's Happening Events, services, and seasonal moments
  • Watch & Listen Live stream and recorded services
  • Prayer Requests Share a prayer with the parish
  • Lockhart Child Development Center Outside site The on-campus preschool; the footer links out to lockhartcdc.org, as the current site does

A few notes for the group:

• “About” groups our who-we-are pages (clergy, history, formation, and the arboretum), the way most Episcopal parish sites organize them.

• “Give” now opens the parish's own Giving page first, which explains the ways to give (including annual estimated giving and Tour de Saints), then links to the secure online giving site (Realm).

• The Memorial Arboretum and Prayer Requests web addresses are kept exactly as they are today, because printed signs and QR codes point to them.

• The top menu is deliberately short. Events and a few utility pages live in the footer, which keeps the navigation simple on phones.

• Links that leave the website (online giving, the preschool) are marked and carry a small ↗ icon so people know they're heading off-site.
