What happens next?

These are working concepts, not final designs. Once the vestry chooses a direction, we can refine the details: adjust colors, swap photos, rewrite copy, add pages. Think of this as choosing the feeling, not the final product.

Both options link to our existing site for deeper content (giving, calendar, member portal). A full transition would happen gradually, not all at once.
