When You Need Us

Pastoral Care

We walk with each other in joy and in sorrow. Reach out at any time, whether you're a member or not.

Need Care Now

Clergy On Call

If you or a loved one needs prayer, a hospital visit, or sacramental care, please contact the parish office at (704) 782-2024. The clergy on call will respond within 24 hours.

For non-urgent prayer requests, use the prayer request form. The prayer team holds the parish and our wider community in prayer each week.

Email the Parish Office (704) 782-2024
Pastoral Care Team

Compassionate, Tangible Support

The Pastoral Care Team seeks to serve members of All Saints' with care that meets real needs, in real life.

Pastoral Visits

Hospital, home, and care-facility visits from clergy and trained lay visitors. Deacon Vern Cahoon coordinates nursing-home visits.

Meals When You Need Them

Home-cooked dishes for those recovering from illness or surgery, families settling in, and parents of newborns. A freezer of prepared meals is kept ready for short-notice needs.

Prayer Requests

Submit a prayer request, public or confidential. The prayer team holds each request in prayer through the week.

Stitches for the Saints

A knitting and crocheting group that creates prayer shawls for hospitalized members, the homebound, and infants. Meets the third Tuesday of each month in the Welcome Center. Beginners welcome.

Communion at Home

Communion brought to the homebound and those who cannot attend services. Contact the parish office to arrange a visit.

Cards, Calls, and Flowers

Greeting cards, follow-up calls, and altar flowers shared with members in the hospital, in recovery, or in seasons of grief.

Tell us how we can walk with you

There is no situation too small or too heavy. Even a quick note that you'd like to be on someone's mind is enough to begin.
