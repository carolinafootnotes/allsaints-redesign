A Way of Following Jesus

What is an Episcopalian?

Ancient prayer, open table, a Church that loves questions as much as answers.

The Episcopal Church is sometimes called a bridge between the Catholic and Protestant traditions. We hold to ancient liturgy and the sacraments, and we read scripture with curiosity, not fear. Our Sunday worship is shaped by music, the Word, prayer, and Holy Communion: a sacred meal that remembers the supper Jesus shared with his disciples.

We are part of the worldwide Anglican Communion. Our parish is rooted in the Episcopal Diocese of North Carolina, and through our diocese we are connected to congregations on every continent.

Everyone is welcome at the table

There are no prerequisites in the Episcopal Church. We welcome people of every age, gender, sexual orientation, race, economic background, and life circumstance. If you are baptized in any Christian tradition, you are welcome to receive Communion with us. If you are not, you are welcome to come forward for a blessing, or to remain in your seat, no pressure either way.

A faith that holds room for questions

Episcopalians follow Jesus Christ. We treat scripture with deep seriousness and read it with intellectual honesty, including the parts that are difficult or that pull against each other. The tradition we inherit is sometimes called the "via media", the middle way: we make room for disagreement and dialogue, knowing every person at the table is beloved of God.

Common prayer is what binds us

We pray from the Book of Common Prayer, a book of liturgies first compiled in 1549 and revised many times since. It means that on a given Sunday, Episcopalians around the world are praying the same words. You don't need to agree on every doctrine to belong here. You need only to come and pray together.

Faith that shows up in the neighborhood

In our baptismal covenant, we promise to seek and serve Christ in all people, to respect the dignity of every human being, and to work for justice and peace. That promise shapes everything from our outreach partnerships in Concord to the way we treat each other on a Sunday morning.
