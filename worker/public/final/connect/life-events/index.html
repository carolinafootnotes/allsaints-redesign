Marriage

Weddings at All Saints' Episcopal

An Episcopal wedding is more than a ceremony. It is a sacrament: a public promise made before God and a community that commits to walk with you. The liturgy is rooted in the Book of Common Prayer, with room for music, scripture, and personal touches that fit you.

We ask that at least one partner be a member of All Saints' before a wedding is scheduled, and we take marriage preparation seriously. Our clergy meet with every couple through a preparation process that's honest, warm, and practical.

Reach out early. We'd rather start the conversation about membership and timing well before a date is set.

Baptism

Baptisms at All Saints' Episcopal

In the Episcopal Church, baptism is a sacrament, a visible sign of the grace already at work in a life. Through baptism, the person being baptized (an infant, a child, a teen, or an adult) is welcomed fully into the Body of Christ and into the life of this parish.

Baptisms typically happen on principal feast days throughout the year: the Baptism of Our Lord, the Easter Vigil, Pentecost, and All Saints' Sunday. We'll walk with parents, godparents, and adult candidates through a short preparation conversation before the day.

If you were baptized as an infant and want to publicly own that faith for yourself, talk to us about confirmation. The bishop visits the parish each year and welcomes those ready to take that step.

Funerals & Memorials

Funerals at All Saints' Episcopal

The Burial Office in the Book of Common Prayer is built on Christian hope: that the life we share with God in this world is not ended at death. The liturgy is honest about grief and steady in its trust. It holds families through one of the hardest days of a life.

All Saints' clergy guide families through every part of planning a service, including readings, hymns, and the role of the parish in the days surrounding the funeral. The parish often provides a reception for families afterward.

If you are facing the loss of someone you love, or thinking ahead about your own service, please reach out. We are here.
