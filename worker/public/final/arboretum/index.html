Memorial Arboretum

Trees of remembrance

A living memorial on the nine-acre All Saints' campus. Each tree carries a name, a story, and the love of the people who planted it.

A living memorial

The Arboretum at All Saints'

Each tree in the arboretum is planted in memory or thanksgiving for someone in the parish family. A small plaque at the base names the person and the species. Scan the QR code on the plaque and you'll find the story online: who they were, who planted the tree, and what kind of tree they chose. The grounds are open to anyone who wants to walk through and remember.
