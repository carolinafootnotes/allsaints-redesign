About this tree

The Shortleaf Pine is a tall and straight tree with a rounded top. It has a long tap root and is therefore quite drought-resistant, growing 50 to 100 feet tall. The color of young bark is greyish, dark and scaly, turning reddish brown with maturity and forming fragmented rectangular plates. Native to the eastern United States, the Shortleaf Pine prefers full sun and loose, acidic soils. The name echinata means “prickly,” referring to small prickles on the cone scales.

Given in memory of Marshall Smith by Debby Smith. Part of the living memorial at All Saints' Memorial Arboretum.
