Two Services. One Community.

Both are Holy Eucharist, Rite II. One is casual and family-friendly; one is traditional with choir and organ. Both are real.

9:00 AM

Family Eucharist

In the Parish Hall. Less formal, family-friendly, with a children's sermon and the kids invited up around the altar at communion. Casual dress. Streamed live on Facebook and YouTube.

11:10 AM

Traditional Eucharist

In the Nave. Traditional hymnody, the Choir of All Saints' with organ, ancient words made new. This is holy ground, and you're invited in. Also livestreamed.

Your First Sunday

The questions guests most often ask before they get here.

Parking

Park right in front of the church. Accessible spaces near the entrance.

What to Wear

Come as you are. Casual at 9 AM. Some dress up for 11:10. No one is judged.

Kids

Nursery for infants and toddlers. Children's sermon at 9 AM. Family-friendly seating in the Parish Hall.

The Service

A bulletin walks you through everything: when to stand, sit, kneel, or stay seated. The Book of Common Prayer is in the pew if you'd like to follow along.

Communion

All who love God and are drawn to Christ are welcome at Christ's table. You don't need to be Episcopalian or a member. Cross your arms for a blessing instead if you prefer.

Your First Visit

No pressure to participate. Greeters meet you at the door. Coffee after the 11:10 service.
