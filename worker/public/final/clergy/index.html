Interim Rector

The Rev. John Johanssen

Fr. John began as our Interim Rector on January 1, 2026. A native of Ohio, he has served faithfully in several parishes throughout his ordained ministry. Before responding to the call to priesthood, he practiced law for several years.

He received his Master of Divinity from Virginia Theological Seminary and has served in a variety of roles, including previous interim work.

Specialized training in interim ministry, equipping him to guide congregations with care and clarity during seasons of transition.

Fun fact: Fr. John's hobbies include music, sailing, hiking, and any activity in nature.

Priest Associate

The Rev. Dr. James B. ("Father Jim") Bernacki

Fr. Jim joined the All Saints' staff in the summer of 2019. A native of the Buffalo, New York area, he graduated from Canisius College with a B.A. in Philosophy and went on to receive an Ed.M. in Secondary Math Education from SUNY Buffalo. With Permanent Certification from the State of New York, he taught math at primary, secondary, and college levels.

He received an M.Div. in Theology from Christ the King Seminary in East Aurora, NY, and a D.Min. in Anglican Studies from Seabury-Western Theological Seminary. Ordained to the priesthood in 1979, he served in Roman Catholic parishes in the Buffalo area until he was received into the Episcopal Church in 1988, when he married his wife Sandy.

He later served congregations in New York, Ohio, and West Virginia, most recently as Rector for Christ Episcopal Church in Albemarle, NC, for over 15 years until retiring in 2018.

At All Saints', Fr. Jim coordinates a team of members who lead Compline every Tuesday at 8 p.m. online, leads or preaches at Sunday and weekday services, and provides pastoral care and visitation.
