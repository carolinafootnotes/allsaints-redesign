A Church That Loves as
God Loves

All Saints' is the Episcopal branch of the Jesus Movement, a faith community where his love, forgiveness, and healing are changing lives.

"God is love, and those who abide in love abide in God, and God abides in them."
1 John 4:16

Whether you are visiting from afar or have lived in Concord your whole life, you are welcome here. We are a faith community where God's love meets you exactly where you are. We've been gathering in Concord since 1878, and the door is wide open for you.

Two Sunday services: 9 AM in the Parish Hall and 11:10 AM in the Nave. Nursery available for infants and toddlers. Accessible entrances on all sides.

Our Mission

Our Vision

To be a church that Loves as God Loves.

Sunday Worship

Two Services, One Community

Both services are Holy Eucharist, Rite II. One is casual and family-friendly; one is traditional with choir and organ.

9:00 AM

Family Eucharist

Holy Eucharist, Rite II, in the Parish Hall. Less formal, family-friendly, with a children's sermon and the kids invited around the altar at communion. Come as you are, really.

11:10 AM

Traditional Eucharist

Holy Eucharist, Rite II, in the Nave. Traditional hymnody, the Choir of All Saints' with organ, ancient words made new. This is holy ground, and you're invited in.

What to expect when you visit

The shape of the service. A bulletin walks you through everything: when to stand, sit, kneel, or stay seated. The Book of Common Prayer is in the pew if you'd like to follow along.
Communion. All who love God and are drawn to Christ are welcome at Christ's table. You don't need to be Episcopalian or a member here. Cross your arms for a blessing if you'd rather not receive.
What to wear. Whatever you're comfortable in. Both services run about an hour to an hour and fifteen minutes.
Children. Children are a full part of worship here. Nursery care is available; formation for kids meets between services.
