Formation

How we learn, together

Christian formation as a lifelong practice, not a program.

Faith, deepening

Formation is the whole life

Christian formation is the lifelong process of growing in relationship with God, with ourselves, with each other, and with all of creation. It happens in classrooms and in cars, at the altar and at the kitchen table. At All Saints', it shows up three ways on Sunday mornings, and one way that takes four years.

Children & Youth Formation

Three layered offerings on Sunday mornings, plus monthly youth gatherings.

  • Nursery (ages 0–3): professionally staffed by Katie, Jane, and Kristen. Additional coverage for ages 3–5 coming soon.
  • Elementary Sunday School (10:00 AM, Education Wing): uses the Dig-In curriculum from Group Publishing, supplemented with units on Advent and Lent.
  • Middle School "Breakfast with Jesus" (Youth Room): a lectionary-based study produced in-house.
  • High School "Coffee with Jesus" (Panera): honest conversation about making faith matter Monday through Saturday.
  • EYC (Episcopal Youth Community): middle and high schoolers gathering for monthly "Invite a Friend!" socials, diocesan retreats, local service, and annual trips.

Adult Formation

A standing Sunday class, in person and on Zoom, with topics that rotate through the program year.

  • When: most Sundays, 10:10–11:00 AM
  • Where: in person at the church and live on Zoom
  • Format: rotating studies through scripture, theology, church history, and contemporary questions
  • Check the Weekly Word for the current study and the Zoom link, or email the office and we'll get you in.

Education for Ministry (EfM)

Every baptized person is called to ministry. EfM is the four-year program that takes that seriously.

  • Offered under the auspices of the University of the South (Sewanee, TN)
  • Four years of study in scripture, church history, and theology
  • Strong emphasis on integrating faith into daily life through theological reflection
  • All Saints' currently hosts two EfM groups
  • A short video introduction is available, ask the office for the link

Have questions about formation?

Tell us what age, what topic, or what time of week works, and we'll point you to the right room or the right person.
