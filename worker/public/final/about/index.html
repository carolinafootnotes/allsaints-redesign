About All Saints'

The people, the history, and the life that make this parish what it is.

All Saints' has been part of Concord since 1878, a parish of the Episcopal branch of the Jesus Movement. We are a community learning, together, to love as God loves: in worship, in formation, in service to our neighbors, and in care for one another. These pages introduce the people, the history, and the everyday life of the parish.
