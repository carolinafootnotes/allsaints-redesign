Thank you for being part of this

Generous giving sustains the work of this parish, our worship and formation, the children at LCDC, the partnerships at CCM, Habitat, and Episcopal Relief & Development, and the daily work of being a neighbor in Concord. Whatever you give, you are part of this.

A few more things to know

Estimated giving, taxes, and Tour de Saints

Estimate of giving
Each year we invite the parish to prayerfully consider an estimate of giving for the year ahead. You can make or update your estimate online through Realm, opens in new tab by tapping “Giving,” where you can also set up a one-time or recurring gift.
Tax benefits
Many of us give out of gratitude rather than for the tax benefits, but there can be tax advantages too. Two that parishioners often use are a gift of appreciated stock, which avoids tax on the gain and is deductible at full value, and, for those required to take distributions from a retirement account, a Qualified Charitable Distribution made directly from an IRA. For either, contact the parish office, and please consult your tax advisor about your own situation.
Tour de Saints
Our annual charity bike ride raises funds and food for Cooperative Christian Ministry (CCM). Find the ride and sponsorship details on the Serve page.

Your giving funds Sunday worship, Christian formation for all ages, the preschool on our campus, our partnerships with CCM and the Free Clinic, and the everyday work of being a neighbor in Concord.
