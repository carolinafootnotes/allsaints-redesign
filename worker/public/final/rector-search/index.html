A Season of Discernment

Our Rector Search

All Saints' is listening for the next priest called to lead this parish.

Where We Are Now

Led by an Interim Rector

The Rev. John Johanssen began as our Interim Rector on January 1, 2026, bringing specialized training in transition ministry and a Master of Divinity from Virginia Theological Seminary.

With Fr. John's steady leadership, the vestry has begun the work of calling our next rector, walking together through the discernment process in partnership with the Episcopal Diocese of North Carolina.

For Clergy Considering This Call

A Parish, in Brief

A snapshot for priests in discernment. The full parish profile is being prepared.

For deeper context, past annual reports are available on our current site.

Rooted since 1878

A historic Episcopal parish in Concord, NC, with deep ties to the Diocese of North Carolina.

Two Sunday liturgies

A family-friendly 9 AM in the Parish Hall and a traditional 11:10 AM in the Nave. Both Rite II.

Five music ensembles

Choir of All Saints', Resurrection Choir, Bells of All Saints', St. Nicholas, and Junior Choirs.

Formation for every age

Children & Youth ministries, adult formation, two Education for Ministry groups.

A real, mature congregation

Established Sunday community, active lay leadership, and a vestry stepping into the search with care.

Open to the conversation

We welcome questions, conversation, and discernment from priests who feel called to consider this parish.

The Process

How a Rector Is Called

  1. 01
    Interim Ministry

    The Rev. John Johanssen leads the parish through the transition, with specialized training in interim and discernment work.

  2. 02
    Discernment & Parish Profile

    The vestry and a discernment committee listen to the parish, name our gifts and needs, and prepare a profile that tells our story honestly.

  3. 03
    Search

    The parish profile and position are published in partnership with the Episcopal Diocese of North Carolina. Candidates are received, interviewed, and discerned with prayer.

  4. 04
    Call & Installation

    A call is extended, the candidate accepts, and a celebration of new ministry seats the next rector at All Saints'.

Talking with the search committee

Clergy considering this call, or parishioners with questions about the process, are warmly invited to reach out. The parish office routes all rector-search correspondence to the right people.
