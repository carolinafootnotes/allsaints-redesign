Parish Life

A Little Less Serious

Not every gathering is a formation opportunity. Some of them are just fun.

Annual / Spring

Tour de Saints

A bike ride fundraiser for Cooperative Christian Ministry. All distances welcome, no spandex required.

Annual / Summer

Marshall Smith Baseball Outing

An afternoon at the ballpark with the parish. A tradition held in memory of Marshall Smith.

Sporadic

Game Night & Trivia

Watch the Weekly Word for the next one. Low stakes, high fun.

Seasonal

Parish Picnics & Cookouts

We find any excuse to cook outdoors together. Summer and fall are the usual suspects.

Annual / Winter

After Dark @ All Saints'

Evening classes taught by parishioners and local experts, from cooking to wine tasting, with proceeds benefiting local organizations like Cooperative Christian Ministry. Each year brings a new lineup, posted with registration when the season opens.

Annual / Summer

Vacation Bible School

A week of stories, songs, crafts, and play for children on our grounds. It wraps up with the kids helping lead worship that Sunday.

Annual / December

Tacky Gift Exchange

Adults gather in the Welcome Center for snacks and a swap of gloriously questionable gifts. Bring something to share and something nobody could possibly want.
