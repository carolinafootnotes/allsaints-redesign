On the All Saints' Campus

Lockhart Child Development Center

Quality early childhood care for children 3 months through 5 years, hosted on the All Saints' campus in Concord.

About LCDC

A nurturing place to grow and learn

Lockhart Child Development Center (LCDC) provides quality, developmentally appropriate child development services for children 3 months through 5 years of age. The center is named for Dr. David A. Lockhart, a Concord pediatrician who spent more than 40 years caring for the children of this community.

LCDC shares the All Saints' Episcopal campus at 525 Lake Concord Road. The school operates independently, with its own staff, leadership, and enrollment, and is open weekdays from 6:30 AM to 5:30 PM.

The teaching team works to provide an enriched, nurturing environment that supports each child's cognitive, language, social, emotional, and physical development, with a strong focus on independence and a positive self-concept.

The Creative Curriculum

A planned, play-based learning environment

LCDC follows the Creative Curriculum, with developmentally appropriate lesson plans implemented weekly across hands-on learning centers.

Free play and learning centers

Children choose from Block, Dramatic Play, Manipulative, Science, Art, Book, and Writing centers that build skills through exploration.

Circle and group times

The most structured part of the day, focused on language development, listening, and group participation.

Art and creativity

Activities designed to stimulate each child's creativity while developing fine motor skills.

Ongoing assessment

Teachers observe and track developmental skills for each child, sharing progress with families at parent-teacher conferences.
