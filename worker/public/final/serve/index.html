How we serve, near and far

Service runs through everything at All Saints'. Some of it is visible on Sunday morning, some of it happens in the gardens, in the community, and on the other side of the world.

Faith in Action

Service is a way of life, not a program

We've organized service into three places it tends to show up: inside our doors, around Concord, and around the world. Real people, real partnerships, going back decades.

Tier One

In the Church

The ongoing service inside our doors. Sunday morning depends on dozens of people quietly showing up, and the campus depends on a few more.

Greeters

Welcoming people on Sunday morning, holding the door, making sure no one walks in alone or unseen.

Ushers

Orienting guests, handing out bulletins, helping with seating and communion flow.

Welcome Desk

Staffing the parish office during the week, answering phones, greeting guests who stop in.

Gardens of All Saints'

Beautifying and stewarding the 9-acre campus, including the cisterns for rainwater and the herb gardens. Contact Shelley Williams.

Events Planning Team

Coordinating fundraising and parish events through the year. Contact Andrea Holdren.

Altar Guild

Preparing the altar, vestments, and vessels for each service. Quiet, careful, weekly work.

Lay Eucharistic Ministers

Assisting at the altar on Sundays and bringing communion to the homebound during the week.

Hospitality

Coffee hour, parish dinners, and the meals that show up when someone needs one.

Worship Support

Lectors, acolytes, intercessors, livestream tech. There's a role for almost any gift.

Tier Two

In the Community

Local partnerships and outreach in Concord. Some of these go back to the founding of the organizations themselves.

Tour de Saints

Our annual charity bike ride, raising funds and food for Cooperative Christian Ministry. Over three years, more than 500 cyclists have ridden and over $20,000 has been raised.

Cooperative Christian Ministry (CCM)

All Saints' was a founding partner. CCM is a Concord nonprofit serving neighbors in need across Cabarrus County through food, shelter, and crisis assistance.

Lockhart Child Development Center

A public preschool on the All Saints' campus, run in partnership with Cabarrus County Schools and the Cabarrus Partnership for Children. Up to 36 four-year-old pre-K students. The All Saints' Early Childhood Foundation (ASECF, 501c3) supports it.

Community Free Clinic

Another founding partner, providing medical care to uninsured and underinsured residents of Cabarrus County.

Cabarrus County Night Shelter

Founding partner of the local night shelter, providing safe overnight housing for neighbors experiencing homelessness.

Lenten Food Drive & Community Meals

Seasonal drives and meals that feed CCM's pantry and bring people together at our table.

Tier Three

In the World

Caring that reaches past Concord, through the wider Episcopal Church. You don't have to leave home to help.

Episcopal Church Partnership

Episcopal Relief & Development

The international relief agency of the Episcopal Church. We support its work responding to disasters, fighting hunger, and strengthening communities around the world.

Global Health

Nets for Life

Mosquito nets distributed to families in malaria-affected regions. A small thing that saves lives.
